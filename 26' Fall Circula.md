# Fall Curriculum Unit Plan

**Web Development, Artificial Intelligence, Radical Social Entrepreneurship, and Leadership**

## Overview

This Fall Quarter, our Geniuses will continue developing their technical and entrepreneurial skills through web development, Artificial Intelligence, Radical Social Entrepreneurship, and Leadership.

Geniuses will focus on HTML/CSS, JavaScript, AI assisted product development, and Flask while building a responsive website connected to their Radical Social Entrepreneurship business.

Artificial Intelligence will be taught as a practical tool that supports the development process. Geniuses will learn how to prompt effectively, use AI for product research and planning, review and debug code, test ideas, improve user experience, and strengthen the overall quality of their product.

The expectation is that AI supports the work rather than completing the work for them. Geniuses should understand the code, decisions, and final product they create.

Geniuses will also continue developing their Radical Social Entrepreneurship businesses by learning how to build financially sustainable ideas, create marketing strategies, develop strong teams, and understand legal structures.

Leadership lessons will continue to focus on personal development, financial literacy, education, global community, the Black diaspora, and manhood development.

# Artificial Intelligence Expectations

The AI unit is designed to support the technical and product development work already happening throughout the quarter.

Geniuses should use AI as a tool for thinking, building, reviewing, testing, and improving their work.

### Geniuses are expected to:

1. Understand any AI generated code used in their project.

2. Test AI generated code before adding it to the final product.

3. Review and verify important information produced by AI.

4. Avoid entering private or sensitive information into AI systems.

5. Make their own final decisions about what should or should not be included in their product.

6. Explain how AI supported their development process.

7. Maintain an AI Development Log throughout the project.

## AI Development Log

Each team will document at least five meaningful examples of how AI was used during the project.

The purpose of the log is not to document every interaction with AI.

The purpose is to show how AI helped the team solve a problem, make a decision, improve their code, or strengthen their product.

# Final Project Expectations

By the end of the quarter, each Genius or team should have a functional Flask based web application connected to a Radical Social Entrepreneurship business.

The final product should demonstrate technical development, a clear target user, a meaningful problem or need, a realistic business direction, and thoughtful use of Artificial Intelligence throughout the development process.

The final project should include:

1. A clear problem or need

2. A defined target user

3. A functional website

4. Meaningful JavaScript functionality

5. Flask integration

6. Database integration

7. A clear RSE business direction

8. Documentation showing how AI supported the development process

9. A final product demonstration

10. A final business and product pitch

# Final Goal

The goal of the Fall Quarter is for Geniuses to understand how to take an idea and develop it into a working product.

Geniuses should leave the quarter with experience using programming, Artificial Intelligence, product development, entrepreneurship, and leadership together.

Artificial Intelligence should strengthen the work being done.

The Genius is still responsible for the idea, the decisions, the code, and the final product.
